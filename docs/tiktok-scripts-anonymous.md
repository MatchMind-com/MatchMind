3. **Font**: One bold sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, or whatever CapCut has — pick once, stick)
4. **Outro card** (2s): "matchmindcom.com/world-cup" + "link in bio" on black

6. **Audio**: Same voice every video (ElevenLabs ID), same outro sting

After 5-6 videos, viewers recognize the brand on sight — the brand identity does the work the face would have done.

- "[MARKET] @ [ODDS]"
- "Three data points stacked vertically"
- "+X% EDGE" (big, orange, hold 2s)
- "Free in bio →"

**Caption template**: "[HOME] vs [AWAY] — the bookies are wrong. AI says [MARKET] @ [ODDS], +[X]% edge. Full reasoning in bio. #[LEAGUE_HASHTAG] #footballbetting #valuebets #aibetting"
