# MatchMind brand workspace — positioning & integration spec

> Captured from conversation 2026-06-05. Should drive landing, social,
> in-app copy, and TikTok scripts going forward.

## The positioning line

> **You're the coach of your own sports betting fund. MatchMind gives
> you the tools to run it.**

This is the brand line. Use everywhere positioning is needed: hero
sub-headline, social bios, pitch decks, TikTok video hooks, email
welcome copy, App Store descriptions.

## What changes about the product framing

MatchMind isn't a "tips site" or "prediction service" — it's the
**workspace** for someone running their bets like a fund:

| Feature | Old framing | New framing |
|---|---|---|
| AI predictions | "Today's picks" | The analyst desk |
| Coach (GPT-4o chat) | "Ask the AI anything" | Your embedded coach |
| Player profiles | (not surfaced) | Roster intel for the manager |
| Odds movement | (not surfaced) | The market signal your fund desk needs |
| Bankroll + Kelly tools | "Stake calculator" | Risk management for your fund |
| Track record | "Our wins/losses" | Your audit log — every position public |
| Bet slip tracker | "Bet history" | Position tracking |

## Audience reframe

- **From**: gambler following tips
- **To**: someone running their own fund — analyst desk, research tool,
  AI assistant, all in one workspace

This shifts MatchMind out of the "punter platform" category and into the
"betting workspace / decision tooling" category. Bigger market, less
crowded, longer LTV.

## Where to apply

1. **Landing page hero** — sub-headline under "Find the edge"
2. **/world-cup hero** — replace the generic "AI analyses every match"
   with the workspace framing where appropriate
3. **Social bios** — Twitter, Instagram, TikTok
4. **OG meta description** — site-wide
5. **Welcome email** (`app/api/subscribe/route.ts` welcome body)
6. **Pricing page tagline** — "Tools for running your fund"
7. **TikTok video closers** — replace "smarter bets" with workspace line

## Tone consistency

- Stay direct, data-led, anti-hype (existing brand guideline)
- Never imply guaranteed wins — coaching framing helps here ("a fund
  manager makes losing trades too")
- 18+ disclaimer + BeGambleAware link still required on all public pages
